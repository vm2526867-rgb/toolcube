Short guides to help you pick the right tool for the job.

  1. Best AI Tools for Students (2026)

    Students use AI to understand topics, organise notes and polish writing. Use these tools to learn, not to copy, and always follow your school's rules on AI.

  2. Best AI Tools for Content Creators (2026)

    Creators need to make more content in less time. These tools help with design, video, voice, music and writing, so you can spend your time on ideas.

  3. Best AI Tools for Small Business (2026)

    Small teams can use AI for support, marketing and admin without hiring a big team. Start with one task and measure the result before adding more.

  4. Best AI Tools for Health and Wellness (2026)

    These tools support fitness, sleep and mental wellbeing. They are helpers, not a doctor, so always check anything medical with a professional.

  5. Best AI Tools for Real Estate and Personal Finance (2026)

    From budgeting to home value estimates, these tools help you understand money and property, but they are not a substitute for a financial advisor or an appraiser.
